\begin{abstract}
This paper draft outlines a case study on AI-assisted storyboarding using generative image tools. The study compares text-only prompting, prompt refinement, and prompt-with-visual-reference strategies in a controlled narrative scenario. The pilot uses three critical scenes from a short adventure story and evaluates narrative alignment, character consistency, visual style consistency, image quality, storyboard usability, and practical control.
\end{abstract}
